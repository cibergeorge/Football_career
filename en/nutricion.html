Nutrition for a Star Footballer

🍎 Your Fuel for the Match

Nutrition is just as important as training. Think of your body as a race car: if you put bad fuel in it, it won't go fast or last the whole circuit. You need the best fuel to be the best on the pitch!

What to Eat to Win?

  • **Carbohydrates (The Slow Fuel):** They are your main source of energy. Eat pasta, rice, potatoes, and whole-wheat bread! This is what allows you to run for 90 minutes.
  • **Protein (The Building Blocks):** They help your muscles repair and grow after a tough training session. Find them in chicken, fish, eggs, legumes, and dairy.
  • **Fruits and Vegetables (The Superpowers):** They give you vitamins and minerals to keep you from getting sick and to keep your bones strong. Think of them as the "maintenance" for your super car!

💧 Hydration: Don't Forget Water!

Water is key. If you are thirsty, it's already too late. Drink water before, during, and after training. It keeps your engine cool and your mind ready to make decisions.
